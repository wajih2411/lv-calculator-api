Results

  • Total bandwidth

    – Mbps

    cameras × bitrate per camera (H.264 at 15 fps)

  • Storage

    – TB

    bandwidth × hours per day × days

  • Total load

    – W

    cameras × power per camera + switch and recorder

  • Heat load

    – BTU/hr

    load × 3.412

  • UPS runtime

    – min

    battery × efficiency ÷ load × 60
